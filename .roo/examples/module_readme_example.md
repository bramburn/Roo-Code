# Example: Module README.md

This is an example of a complete Module README.md file following MTM principles.

```markdown
# Module 01: Foundation

## Module Overview
This foundational module introduces learners to Mandarin Chinese through the Michel Thomas Method approach. Students will establish psychological comfort, discover cognates and familiar patterns, and learn essential building blocks including personal pronouns, basic greetings, and the fundamental verb "to be." This module creates the confidence and understanding necessary for all subsequent learning.

## Learning Objectives
By completing this module, students will be able to:
- Understand the psychological principles behind stress-free language learning
- Recognize cognates and familiar patterns between English and Mandarin
- Use personal pronouns (I, you, he, she, we, they) correctly
- Greet others and respond to greetings appropriately
- Express basic courtesy and politeness
- Count from 1 to 10
- Tell time and discuss days of the week
- Construct simple sentences using the verb "to be"

## Key Concepts
- **Grammar Points:** Personal pronouns, present tense "to be", basic sentence structure, word order
- **Vocabulary Themes:** Greetings, courtesy expressions, numbers, days, time expressions
- **Communication Skills:** Greeting others, introducing oneself, basic politeness

## Lessons

| Lesson | Title | Grammar Point | Key Vocabulary | Duration |
|--------|-------|---------------|----------------|----------|
| 01 | Psychological Setup & Cognates | Sentence structure basics | Cognates, greetings | 5 min |
| 02 | First Verb - "To Be" | Present tense 是 | Common verbs, pronouns | 8 min |
| 03 | Personal Pronouns | Pronoun forms | I, you, he, she, we, they | 7 min |
| 04 | Common Greetings | Greeting patterns | Hello, goodbye, thanks | 6 min |
| 05 | Basic Courtesy | Politeness markers | Please, thank you, sorry | 6 min |
| 06 | Numbers 1-10 | Number formation | One through ten | 7 min |
| 07 | Days of the Week | Day names | Monday through Sunday | 6 min |
| 08 | Time Expressions | Time telling | Hours, minutes, time markers | 7 min |
| 09 | Foundation Review | All concepts | All vocabulary | 8 min |
| 10 | Foundation Consolidation | Integration | Mixed practice | 10 min |

## MTM Approach in This Module

This module strictly adheres to the Michel Thomas Method principles:

### Teacher Responsibility
The instructor takes 100% responsibility for student learning. Students are never asked to memorize or "try hard." If confusion arises, it's treated as the teacher's responsibility to clarify.

### One New Concept at a Time
Each lesson introduces either a new grammar concept OR new vocabulary, never both simultaneously. For example:
- Lesson 02 introduces the verb "to be" using only known pronouns
- Lesson 03 introduces new pronouns using only the known verb "to be"

### The Bridge
Lesson 01 focuses on finding familiar patterns and cognates between English and Mandarin, immediately building confidence that the languages share common ground.

### Stress-Free Environment
- No memorization required
- No homework
- No pressure to perform
- Relaxed, conversational tone throughout

### Ladder of Success
Each lesson builds naturally on previous learning. Students never encounter structures they haven't been prepared for.

## Prerequisites
Students should have:
- Basic English proficiency
- Willingness to listen actively without taking notes
- Openness to a conversational learning approach
- No prior Mandarin knowledge required

## What Comes Next
After completing this module, students will move to **Module 02: Questions**, where they will learn how to form questions and ask for information. The foundation built here will enable them to ask about people, things, locations, and times.

## Tips for Success
- Listen actively without taking notes - the teacher handles all responsibility
- Pause and produce when prompted - speak out loud during pause moments
- Don't worry about perfect pronunciation - focus on understanding
- Review previous lessons if needed - there's no rush
- Trust the process - understanding comes naturally through the structured progression

## Module Statistics
- **Total Lessons:** 10
- **Estimated Duration:** 70 minutes
- **New Vocabulary:** ~50 words
- **Grammar Concepts:** 4 major concepts
- **Recommended Pace:** 2-3 lessons per week
```
